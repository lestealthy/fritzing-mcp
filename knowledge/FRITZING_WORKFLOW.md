# Fritzing Workflow

1. Read `fritzing://rules`.
2. `fritzing_status` to confirm the environment.
3. Search parts with `fritzing_search_parts`.
4. Inspect connectors with `fritzing_get_part_connectors`.
5. `fritzing_create_project`.
6. `fritzing_place_part` for each component.
7. `fritzing_wire` only verified connectors.
8. `fritzing_validate_project`.
9. `fritzing_render` and `fritzing_visual_report`.
10. Fix and re-validate.
11. `fritzing_save_project`.
